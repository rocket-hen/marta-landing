Marta is a service, not a physical product — there is nothing to ship back. This page explains when you're entitled to a refund and how to request one. It's a summary of the fuller terms in Sections 6–8 of our [Terms of Service](/terms/); where the two differ, the Terms govern.

Contact: [support@callmarta.com](mailto:support@callmarta.com)

## 1. Right of withdrawal (consumers in the EU / EEA)

If you're a consumer resident in the EU or EEA, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within **14 days** without giving a reason.

Because our service is time-critical, when you place an order we ask you to **expressly request that we begin immediately**. If you do:

- you can still withdraw within 14 days, but you pay for the part of the service already performed, in proportion to what's been delivered;
- once the service has been **fully performed** — for Blitz, once we've completed the calls and delivered your report — the right of withdrawal is lost.

To withdraw, email [support@callmarta.com](mailto:support@callmarta.com) with a clear statement to that effect. We refund within 14 days of being notified, using the same payment method you used to pay.

## 2. Our 24-hour guarantee (Blitz)

For **Blitz** orders, we commit to delivering your first report — whether we reached the landlord, what they said, and whether a viewing was arranged — within **24 hours** of receiving your listings and the information we need from you.

If we miss that window, email us and **we refund your payment in full.**

The guarantee covers delivery of the report. It does not guarantee that a viewing gets arranged, or that a landlord answers, responds, or accepts you as a tenant — those depend on people we don't control. It doesn't apply where the delay is caused by information you haven't given us, listings that turn out to be unusable (expired, duplicated, no contactable number), or a request outside the plan's scope.

## 3. Marathon and Concierge

Marathon and Concierge don't carry the 24-hour guarantee above — Marathon runs for up to 90 days and Concierge is scoped individually, so a fixed delivery window doesn't apply the same way. Both are covered by the general case-by-case policy in Section 4 below.

## 4. Refunds outside the guarantee

Beyond Sections 1 and 2, we handle refund requests case by case:

- **We refund** where the service failed on our side — calls not made, a report not delivered, an appointment marked as booked that we never actually arranged.
- **We don't refund** where the service was delivered as described and the market simply didn't go your way — a landlord who didn't reply, a flat that got rented to someone else, requirements you didn't meet. That's the nature of a competitive rental market, not a failure of the service.

## 5. How to request a refund

Email [support@callmarta.com](mailto:support@callmarta.com) with your order details and the reason. We aim to respond within 5 working days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method, usually within 14 days.

## 6. No subscriptions to cancel

Blitz and Marathon are one-time purchases, not subscriptions — nothing renews automatically, so there's no recurring charge to cancel. Each plan simply runs until its term ends or you've found your home, whichever comes first.
